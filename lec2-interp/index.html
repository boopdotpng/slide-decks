what changes at scale

every position is graded
every row of every attention matrix is used, not just the last. the rows we threw away do real work
nobody hands out jobs
we added one piece per task, each with one job. gpt-2 gets 144 heads at once: few have a job that clean, most share or split one
more features than numbers
task 3's neurons each fired on sets of digits. in gpt-2 one neuron fires on many unrelated things: the stream packs in more than 768 features
knockouts are blunt
zero a gpt-2 head and you don't know everything it was doing: most heads do several things, and others step in for it (backup heads)
the circuits are still there
a previous-token head feeding a matching head is task 2: gpt-2's induction circuit (olsson et al. 2022). found with the same tools: knock out, probe, read QK and V
